IMPORTANT - email codes (OTP):
The login/signup page asks for a code, so the Supabase email templates must show
{{ .Token }}, not {{ .ConfirmationURL }}. In Authentication → Emails → Templates,
replace the body of BOTH templates with the files in supabase/templates/:
  Magic Link      ← supabase/templates/magic-link.html
  Confirm signup  ← supabase/templates/confirm-signup.html
On new Free projects, custom SMTP (e.g. Resend) must be set up first before
templates can be edited. See supabase/passwordless-auth-setup.md.
